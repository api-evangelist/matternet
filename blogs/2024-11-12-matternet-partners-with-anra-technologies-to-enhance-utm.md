Matternet, a global leader in urban drone delivery, today announced a strategic partnership with ANRA Technologies, an industry leader in Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) Traffic Management (UTM) solutions. This collaboration aims to support the safe and efficient scaling of Matternet’s drone networks as the company accelerates its innovative home delivery services.
