TAMPA, Fla. and MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif., September 15, 2026 – AVI-SPL , the world's leading provider of digital workplace technology solutions, today announced a partnership with Matternet , the world's only FAA Type-Certified drone delivery platform, to deploy drone logistics to speed service delivery across metropolitan markets. The partnership pairs AVI-SPL's service, integration, and supply chain network with Matternet's autonomous drone technology to deliver critical equipment and replacement parts to field technicians in minutes, reducing delays in congested urban and suburban areas.
